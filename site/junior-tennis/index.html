Sound familiar?

When the match gets tight, the mental game takes over

Most junior players have the strokes. What holds them back is what happens between the ears.

Tight in close matches

Plays freely early, then gets tentative at 5–5 or in a tiebreak.

Mistakes snowball

One bad call or missed shot turns into a string of lost points.

Practice vs. tournaments

Looks great in lessons, but can’t bring that level to tournament play.

Negative self-talk

Harsh words, racquet frustration, or giving up on points.

Results pressure

Worrying about rankings, seeding or who they’re playing instead of the next point.

Struggles to close

Builds a lead, then gets nervous and lets the match slip away.

Your coach

Coached by someone who knows competitive tennis

Josh was an all-conference tennis player at Clark University, where he earned his B.A. in psychology, and holds a master’s degree in sport psychology from California State University, Long Beach, where he researched mental toughness.

He has coached tennis at the International Tennis Hall of Fame and at Division I Sacred Heart University, and co-hosts the Tennis IQ Podcast. He teaches players the same mental skills used at the highest levels of the game.
