Scheduling

Sessions will be scheduled based on the client’s and the Sport Psychology Coach’s schedule, and can be scheduled multiple times per week, weekly, bi-weekly, or in strategic moments (such as right before an event). Sessions purchased in packages never expire, though it is recommended to establish a consistent schedule of sessions.

Cancellations

The client must give a minimum of 24 hours notice to cancel or reschedule a scheduled appointment or they will be charged in full, except for cases of sickness and emergencies. The Sport Psychology Coach will wait 15 minutes from the start of a scheduled session before the client is considered a “no show”.
