FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Answers to common questions about working with Josh. Don't see yours? Call or text (203) 814-0342 or email Josh@TiebreakerPsych.com.

What is sport psychology coaching?

Sport psychology coaching helps individual athletes and teams perform at higher levels by breaking down barriers to success and developing mental skills that can be learned and practiced like physical skills. Many athletes find they can use the same skills in the classroom and the workplace too.

Who do you work with?

Josh works with athletes of all sports, ages, and levels, as well as teams and organizations. He has worked with NCAA Division I, II, and III teams, high school teams, and a number of other groups. If an athlete is under 18, their parent should inquire on their behalf.

What can I work on?

Common topics include self-talk, managing emotions during competition, goal-setting, routines, preparation for competitions, and reflecting on competitions, including journaling. For teams, topics include leadership development, communication, handling losses and setbacks, and team goal-setting.

What happens in the free 30-minute intro session?

It's a chance to tell Josh a bit about yourself and what you'd like to work on. Sign up by calling or texting Josh, emailing him, or using the form on the homepage, and you'll hear back to schedule a time.

How do one-on-one sessions work?

Josh creates a customized sport psychology game plan based on your core values, goals, age, and athletic level. Sessions typically start with a check-in on recent performances: what went well, what you want to improve, and how you've applied your mental skills. Each session then focuses on one or two skills to take your game to the next level.

How long are sessions?

Full sessions are 60 minutes and half-sessions are 30 minutes.

Where do sessions take place?

Sessions are virtual, held over Zoom, so you can work with Josh from anywhere.

Do you work with teams and organizations?

Yes. Teams and organizations take part in workshops and exercises designed to teach sport psychology skills and enhance team cohesion. A personalized game plan is designed for each group.

Can you speak to my team, club, or organization?

Yes. Josh speaks to athletes, coaches, parents, clubs, and organizations about the mental side of sport. Use the “Invite Josh to speak” button on the Media page.

How much does it cost?

The first 30-minute introductory session is free. For details and pricing on one-on-one and team sessions, please inquire via email or call/text.
