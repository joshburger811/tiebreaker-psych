Privacy Policy for Tiebreaker Psych LLC

Effective date: November 16, 2026

Tiebreaker Psych (“we,” “us,” or “our”) provides sport psychology coaching to athletes, parents, and teams. This policy explains what information we collect when you visit tiebreakerpsych.com, contact us, or respond to our ads, and how we use and protect it.

This policy covers our website, forms, ads, phone calls, texts, and emails. It does not cover the confidential records of coaching sessions, which are handled separately and discussed with each client.

Information we collect

Information you give us:

  • Website and ad forms: your name, email, phone number, and anything you tell us about the athlete (such as sport, age, and what you’d like help with). This includes forms on our site and Google lead forms shown in our ads.
  • Calls, texts, and emails: your phone number or email address and the content of your message.
  • Booking and sessions: scheduling details you provide when booking an intro session or coaching.

Information collected automatically:

  • Website usage: pages visited, device and browser type, approximate location, and how you arrived at our site, collected through cookies and similar tools.
  • Ad activity: whether you clicked one of our Google ads and then contacted us, so we can measure which ads work. Google reports calls from our ads (such as call length and the caller’s area code) to us.

How we use your information

We use your information to:

  • Respond to your inquiry and schedule your free intro session
  • Provide and manage coaching services
  • Follow up about services you asked about (you can ask us to stop at any time)
  • Measure and improve our website and ads
  • Meet legal obligations

What we don’t do: We do not sell your personal information. We do not use what you tell us about an athlete’s mental health or performance concerns for advertising or ad targeting.

How we share information

We share information only with service providers that help us run our business, and only as needed for them to do that work:

  • The companies that host our website and deliver its contact form
  • Google, which runs our ads and lead forms and measures their results
  • Zoom, for virtual sessions
  • The scheduling, email, and payment tools we use to book, communicate about, and bill sessions

We may also share information if required by law, to protect someone’s safety, or as part of a sale or transfer of our business.

Cookies and advertising

Our website uses cookies to keep it working, understand how visitors use it, and measure whether our Google ads lead to inquiries. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings; some site features may not work without them.

To control how Google uses your information for ads, visit Google’s ad settings or learn more at How Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Athletes under 18

Our website and ads are meant for adults. If an athlete is under 18, a parent or guardian should contact us on their behalf. We do not knowingly collect information online from children under 13; if we learn we have, we will delete it.

Security and retention

We use reasonable safeguards to protect your information, but no website or online service is completely secure. We keep inquiry information only as long as needed to respond, provide services, and meet legal or professional record-keeping requirements.

Your choices

  • Ask us what information we have about you, or ask us to correct or delete it
  • Tell us to stop follow-up calls, texts, or emails at any time (reply STOP to any text)
  • Depending on where you live, you may have additional privacy rights under state law

To make a request, contact us using the details below. We will respond within 30 days.
