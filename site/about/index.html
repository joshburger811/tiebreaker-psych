About

Josh Burger, M.S.

Josh specializes in teaching sport psychology skills that are applicable to athletes and performers of all ages, sports, and levels.

M.S. Sport Psychology
California State University, Long Beach
B.A. Psychology
Clark University · All-conference tennis

Josh knows the mental side of sport from the inside. As an all-conference tennis player at Clark University, where he earned a Bachelor’s Degree in psychology, he learned firsthand how much confidence, focus and composure shape what happens in competition.

That experience led him to California State University, Long Beach, where he earned a Master’s Degree in sport psychology and researched mental toughness in college athletes. He has also coached tennis at the International Tennis Hall of Fame in Newport, RI and at the Division I level at Sacred Heart University in Fairfield, CT, and he co-hosts the Tennis IQ Podcast, which focuses on the mental side of tennis and sport performance.

Today, through sport psychology and mental skills training, Josh helps individuals and teams of all sports, ages and levels perform at a higher level and find more fulfillment in their sport.

Josh is excited to share his knowledge and experience with you to help you master the mental side of the game!
