Services

Sport psychology coaching for individuals and teams

Mental skills can be learned and practiced like physical skills. Choose one-on-one coaching or a program designed for your team.

One-on-one

Individual Sport Psychology

Work one on one with Josh to develop a customized sport psychology game plan based on your core values, goals, age, and athletic level. Topics that we may cover include effective self-talk, managing emotions during competitions, goal-setting, how to utilize routines, preparation for competitions, and reflection on competitions including journaling.

Sessions typically start with a check-in, where Josh talks through recent performances with the athlete including elements of the performance that went well, things that they want to improve, and how they’ve managed to apply sport psychology skills and mental skills while they’re performing. From that point, each session focuses on one or two skills that are needed in order to take their game to the next level.

  • Self-talk
  • Managing emotions
  • Goal-setting
  • Routines
  • Competition prep
  • Journaling

Teams & organizations

Group Sport Psychology

Josh has worked with a number of groups and teams including NCAA Division I, II, & III collegiate sports teams, high school teams, as well as a number of other teams and organizations.

Teams and organizations take part in workshops and exercises designed to teach sport psychology skills and enhance team cohesion. A personalized sport psychology game plan will be designed for each group; topics include leadership development, effective communication strategies, handling losses and setbacks, and goal-setting as a team.

  • Leadership development
  • Communication
  • Handling setbacks
  • Team goal-setting
  • Team cohesion
